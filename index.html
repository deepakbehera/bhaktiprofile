About

A little about me

I am a student of Class 9, Section A at Global City International School, Bengaluru. I am a curious learner who loves to explore both the creative and the logical sides of the world.

When I'm not in class, you'll usually find me with a brush, a pencil or a sketchbook in my hand — turning ideas into colours and lines. At the same time, I really enjoy the challenge of Mathematics, where every problem is a puzzle waiting to be solved, and English, where words can paint pictures of their own.
